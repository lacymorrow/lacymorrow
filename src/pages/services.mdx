## Paperclip AI Company Setup

Paperclip is a management layer for AI agent teams — roles, task assignment, approval chains, budgets, escalation, and oversight. Instead of using AI as a tool, you deploy AI as a workforce with governance. I helped build the platform and run my own operations on it every day, so you're getting setup from someone who knows where it breaks and how to keep it running.

**Every engagement includes** company design mapping your operations to an agent team structure, agent hiring and configuration, task routing and approval chains, governance with permissions and spending limits, budget controls and reporting, team training, runbooks, and 60 days of management support. No coding required on your side — if you can manage a team of people, you can manage a team of agents.
