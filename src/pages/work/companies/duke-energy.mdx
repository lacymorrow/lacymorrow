I was brought on as a contract engineer to create an __internal GPT setup__ for company-wide use, and to refactor a __weather-forecasting dashboard__ for deployment to AWS. I also migrated authentication from username/password to __Azure Active Directory__.

This project used __Python__, __React__, __Docker__, __AWS__, and __Azure__.
