## About

> Site rebuild and ongoing search work for a Charlotte construction management and remodeling firm, where the website is the only sales channel.

RCB sells projects that start in the five figures, and almost every lead arrives through search. That makes the site less of a brochure and more of a sales floor. The brief was to make the work look as good as it is and make the quote request impossible to miss.

The portfolio runs on Payload collections, so RCB adds a finished job by uploading photos and typing a description. No developer involved, which is the only kind of portfolio that stays current.

## The part most contractor sites skip

A site nobody can find is decorative. So the engagement also covers search work and a monthly report pulled from Google Analytics and Search Console, written in plain language: which queries brought people in, and what they did next.

That report has turned out to be the most useful thing in the whole engagement. It turns "is the website working" from an argument into a number, and it surfaces problems that would otherwise sit unnoticed for months.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16 &middot; React 19 &middot; Payload CMS 3 &middot; Drizzle &middot; Postgres &middot; Tailwind &middot; Resend &middot; GA4 and Search Console reporting
