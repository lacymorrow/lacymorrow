## About

> Site rebuild and ongoing search work for a Charlotte construction management and home remodeling firm, where the website is the only sales channel.

RCB sells projects that start at five figures, and nearly every lead arrives through search. That makes the website less of a brochure and more of a sales floor, so the brief was straightforward: make the work look as good as it is, and make the quote request impossible to miss.

The portfolio runs on Payload collections, so RCB adds a finished job by uploading photos and typing a description. No developer involved, which is the only version of a portfolio that stays current.

## The part most sites skip

A contractor site that nobody can find is decorative. The engagement includes ongoing search work and a monthly report pulled from Google Analytics and Search Console that says, in plain language, which queries brought people in and what they did next.

That report has been the most useful artifact of the whole engagement. It turns "is the website working" from an argument into a number, and it catches problems that would otherwise sit unnoticed for months.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16 · React 19 · Payload CMS 3 · Drizzle · Postgres · Tailwind · Resend · GA4 and Search Console reporting
