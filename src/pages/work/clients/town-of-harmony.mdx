## About

> A full rebuild of the municipal site for a town of about 500 people in Iredell County, on a stack the staff can run themselves.

Harmony has no IT department. The old site was a static page that only changed when somebody paid to have it changed, so it mostly didn't change, and meeting agendas went up late or not at all.

Everything the town actually updates now sits behind a CMS: notices, events, agendas, council listings, resident resources. Staff post their own agendas. The emergency bar across the top of the page can be live in under a minute, which is the whole reason it's there.

Most municipal sites are laid out like the org chart. This one is laid out around what people arrive wanting, so the first four cards are report an issue, find an agenda, see what's happening, and reach somebody.

The other constraint was that nobody would be around to babysit it. Every editable surface is a Payload collection with a form written in plain language, no markdown and no shortcodes to remember. It runs on Postgres behind a CDN with transactional mail through Resend, and it has stayed up without incident.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16 &middot; React 19 &middot; Payload CMS 3 &middot; Drizzle &middot; Postgres &middot; Tailwind &middot; Resend
