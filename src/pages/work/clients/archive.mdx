# Earlier Client Work

<Lead>Freelance and agency projects from 2010 to 2013, kept online because the work was real and the lessons stuck.</Lead>

This is where the career started: LAMP stacks, WordPress themes, and custom PHP content management built for local businesses, film production companies, and nonprofits around Boone and Charlotte, North Carolina.

The technology has aged. The problem has not. Every one of these was a business with no technical staff that needed a thing on the internet to work, which is still a fair description of the job.
