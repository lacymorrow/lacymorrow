# Prescription Finder

<Lead>When your pharmacy is out of your medication, it calls the other pharmacies near you and tells you which one has it.</Lead>

<Lead>**[prescriptionfinder.app ↗](https://prescriptionfinder.app)**</Lead>

## About

You enter the medication, the dose and your ZIP code. It lists the open pharmacies nearby for free. For $4.99 it calls them one at a time with an automated voice, asks if they have it in stock, and stops at the first one that does. If nobody picks up, you get your money back.

I built it after watching people lose whole afternoons on hold during the ADHD medication shortage.
